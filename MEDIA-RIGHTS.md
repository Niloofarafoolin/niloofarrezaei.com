# Media rights ledger

Every image, figure or video on the site needs a row here before it is added. No row, no image.

For each asset, record: where it came from, who or what is in it, and the basis for showing it publicly.

- **Photos with identifiable people.** Study participants are never shown identifiably. A photo from a private or informal setting needs each person's agreement. A photo of a public event, such as an award stage or a posed conference group, goes on the site at the owner's decision, and its row records that decision. If a person shown asks for a photo to come down, it comes down.
- **Figures from published papers** need the publisher's license checked. If reuse is not clearly permitted, redraw the figure.
- **No stock images, and no AI-generated illustrations or figures.** The portrait is the owner's own picture and the owner's own choice.
- **Unpublished work gets no figure of its details.** A figure may show only what a published paper or a public presentation reports.
